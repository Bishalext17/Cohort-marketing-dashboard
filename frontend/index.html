Technical Reference & Handbook Rules, formulas, and architecture guidelines

1 · The Cohort Rule

A lead belongs to the cohort of the date it was captured, permanently. For cohort period Dn, count every event that happened between the capture date and capture date + n days, cumulatively. D3 is not "what happened on day 3" — it is everything from day 0 through day 3. Till date runs from capture date to refresh timestamp.

2 · What Moves with Cohort Period vs What Remains Fixed at D0

Moves: Demos booked, scheduled, attended, attendance %, conversions, conversion %, new revenue, ARPU, ROAS.
Fixed at D0: Spend, impressions, clicks, contacts registered, CTR, CPL. Switching from D0 to D30 leaves total spend unchanged.

3 · The Maturity Gate

For a selected Dn, a capture date is included only if capture date + n ≤ refresh date. Incomplete windows are dropped completely to avoid artificial performance drops.

4 · Recalculated Totals

Volume columns are summed. Every ratio (CPL, Attendance %, Conversion %, ARPU, CTR, ROAS) is recalculated from the summed numerator and denominator. Ratios are never averaged.
