⚠️ Key Bottleneck: Android Attendance
🍎 Apple iOS Traffic 2.6× Higher ROAS
• Booking Rate: 60.8%
• Attendance Rate: 32.9% of Booked
• Lead → Paid Conv: 12.8% (ARPU: ₹44,200)
🤖 Google Android Traffic Attendance Drop Leak
• Booking Rate: 59.4% (Equal to iOS)
• Attendance Rate: 25.1% (-7.8% gap)
• Lead → Paid Conv: 4.9% (ARPU: ₹38,600)
💡 CRO Recommendation: Since Android users book at the same rate (~60%), the audience intent is solid. The drop occurs at classroom entry. Action: Deploy 1-click WhatsApp Join Links and Google Calendar auto-sync hooks 15 mins prior to class to reduce browser redirect drop-offs on Android. Potential impact: +₹8.5L/mo incremental revenue.
